Delete your Mill Mate account

Mill Mate, the internal purchase-order app of Alagiri Paper Mills / Alagiri Duplex

Mill Mate accounts are created by the company's administrator for staff. You can ask for your account and personal data to be deleted at any time, whether or not you still work with us.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to enquire@alagiripapermills.com with the subject "Mill Mate account deletion".
  2. Include your Mill Mate username and your name. Send it from the email address or phone number saved on your account, so we can confirm it is you.
  3. We confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days, and reply when it is done.

If you are still employed, you can also ask your Mill Mate administrator, who can delete the account directly in the app.

What is deleted

  • Your login (username and password).
  • Your profile: name, email address, phone number, department and profile picture.
  • Your notification and WhatsApp alert settings.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Orders, approvals, delivery records and attached documents (for example LR copies and proof of receipt) are the company's business records. They are kept as long as the company needs them for its accounts and legal records, and may still show the name of the person who created or approved them.
  • Activity log entries (who changed what, and when) are kept for the same reason.
  • Database backups are overwritten on a rolling basis; deleted account data disappears from backups within 90 days.

Deleting only some data

You can change or remove your email, phone number and profile picture yourself in the app's Settings. For anything else, email us at the address above.
