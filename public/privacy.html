Mill Mate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Mill Mate is the internal purchase and order-tracking app of Alagiri Paper Mills ("we", "us"). It is used only by our employees and administrators to request materials, place orders with suppliers and track deliveries. Accounts are created by our administrators; the public cannot sign up.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, username, work email, mobile number, department, role, and an optional profile picture.
  • Order information: material requests, quantities, purchase orders, delivery dates, status changes and remarks.
  • Photos and documents you attach: for example purchase documents, lorry receipts (LR copies) and proof-of-receipt photos. The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to attach a file.
  • Supplier contact details (company name, contact person, phone, WhatsApp number, email, address) and the WhatsApp messages exchanged with suppliers about availability and delivery dates.
  • Activity records: an audit log of actions in the app (who changed what and when), used for accountability within the company.

We do not collect location, contacts from your phone, advertising identifiers, or browsing activity outside the app.

How we use it

  • To run the purchasing process: approvals, purchase orders, supplier communication, deliveries and receipts.
  • To show notifications about orders inside the app.
  • To keep the app secure, for example signing you in and limiting repeated wrong passwords.

We do not sell your information, show advertising, or use it for any purpose outside the company's purchasing work.

Who can see it

  • Authorised Mill Mate users within Alagiri Paper Mills, according to their role.
  • Suppliers receive only the order details we send them (items, quantities, purchase order number) through WhatsApp.
  • Our service providers, who process data only on our behalf: Amazon Web Services (servers and file storage) and Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business messaging with suppliers).

Storage and security

Data is stored on Amazon Web Services servers and is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Files are kept in private encrypted storage and are opened only through short-lived links. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes.

How long we keep it

Order and audit records are kept for as long as the company needs them for its business and legal records. When a user leaves, an administrator disables or deletes the account.

Your choices

You can update your name, email, phone and profile picture in the app's Settings. To ask for a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion of your account, contact us at the address below.
